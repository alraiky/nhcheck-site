NHCheck Lite collects nothing

No account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere, and there is no server behind it to send anything to.

What is collected

Nothing. NHCheck Lite does not ask you to sign in, does not collect or transmit personal information, does not upload results, does not read your contacts, photos, files or position, and does not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on the device

Your language choice, so it is remembered next time. That is all, and it goes when you uninstall the app. Results are held only while the app is open.

What a run sends

The app measures your connection, so a run puts traffic on the network. Only when you press Run, and only this:

Your routerEcho requests that never leave your own network
The first public router past itProbes to find it, then echo requests to measure it
A DNS resolverOne name lookup, to time it. No page is fetched.
CloudflareFiller data down and up for eight seconds each, to measure throughput
A public-address serviceAn empty request whose reply is your public IP, shown on your own screen

Any server you contact sees the public address the request came from — that is true of every app on your phone. Nothing identifying you is attached: no identifier, no device name, no account, no results.

The location permission

Android requires it before the system will disclose the connected Wi-Fi network's name or return scan results, because a list of nearby networks can be used to infer position. The app asks for it for that reason alone: it never requests a location fix and never touches GPS. Decline it and every check still runs. You can withdraw it in Settings → Apps → NHCheck Lite → Permissions.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction, deletion and portability apply to personal data that has been collected. None is, so there is nothing to disclose, export or erase.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app contacts or keeps, this page is updated before that version is released, and the date above changes with it.
