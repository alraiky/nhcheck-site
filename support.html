Support

Get help

Write to info@nhcheck.net in English or Arabic. Replies usually within a couple of days.

Bug reports

Include the device and Android version, the app version from About, which check misbehaved, the figure it showed and the verdict it gave. A screenshot helps. No logs are collected, so there are none to send.

Common answers

No network name or channel advice Android hides both until the Location permission is granted. Every other check works without it.
Speed test says rate-limited The measurement service limits repeated runs from one address. Wait a few minutes.
ISP gateway "not measured" Normal — those routers often ignore probes. It is not a fault, and the other checks are unaffected.
Figures differ from another app This one measures steady state over parallel streams, after discarding the warm-up.
