NHCheck Lite
Five checks, one page.

Your connection is slow. NHCheck Lite runs five real measurements in about thirty seconds and names which one is at fault — the Wi-Fi in the room, the router, the line out of the house, or the name lookup. Free, on Android.

The five checks

Wi-Fi statusSignal, channel, interference and security — and the clearest channel to move to
LAN gatewayEight echoes to your router: a fault here is inside the house
ISP gatewayThe first public hop past it: a fault here is on the line
DNS lookupA name resolved and timed
Speed testReal download and upload, eight seconds each

Every check performs a real network operation — nothing is simulated. Each ends in PASS, WARN or FAIL, and whatever fell short comes with the fix behind it.
